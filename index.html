Lend Manager – Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Lend Manager app for Android and iPhone, published by Innodhara.

In short: Lend Manager keeps your loan records only on your phone. We don't run servers, we have no accounts, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. We never receive, sell or share your loan or contact data. The only automatic connection is to check your subscription, which sends purchase details only (section 6). Anything else leaves your phone only when you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

1. Who we are

Lend Manager is a personal record-keeping app for money you lend to, or borrow from, people you know. It does not lend money, arrange loans or connect you with lenders. In this policy, "we" means Innodhara, the developer of the app. Contact details are in section 11.

2. Data the app stores on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Loan records: amounts, currency, interest rate, start and return dates, schedules, payments, write-offs and notes about calculations.
  • The other person's details: the name, one phone number and one email address you chose for each loan.
  • Settings: your default currency, reminder and app-lock preferences, and your backup folder choice.
  • Backup password: if you set one, it is kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain).
  • Diagnostic log: a short list of technical events, such as "app started" or an error type, with app version and system version. It contains no names, amounts, dates or contact details.

3. Contacts

  • Android: the app does not ask for permission to read your contacts. When you choose a person, your phone's own Contacts app opens and passes Lend Manager only the single phone number or email you tap.
  • iPhone: with your permission, the app reads your contacts so you can search and choose a person. It keeps only the name, number and email you choose for a loan; the rest of your contacts are never stored or sent anywhere.

5. When data leaves your phone – only when you choose

  • Backups: backups are encrypted on your phone with AES-256 using a password you set, then saved where you choose (for example a folder, Google Drive or iCloud Drive). We cannot read them and cannot recover a forgotten password.
  • Exports and printing: PDF statements and CSV files are not encrypted. The app tells you this first and hands them to your phone's print or share sheet; you choose where they go.
  • Reminders to the other person: Message and WhatsApp open those apps with a reminder text for you to review and send. Call opens your phone's dialler. Those apps' own privacy policies apply to anything you send through them.
  • Diagnostics: if you choose Settings → Send diagnostics, the app shows you the report first, then opens the share sheet so you can send it, for example by email. The report contains no personal data.

6. Subscriptions

Lend Manager is free for the first 2 months, then needs a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store. Without one, you can still view, back up, restore and export everything you recorded.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Checking your subscription: the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to confirm with Google or Apple whether you have an active subscription. For this it sends an anonymous app user ID created by the app, the product you bought and the store's purchase receipt. It sends no loan, contact, amount or name.
  • Google, Apple and RevenueCat process this purchase information under their own privacy policies: Google, Apple, RevenueCat.
  • The free trial's start date is stored on your phone and in your backups, so restoring a backup doesn't start a new trial.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store your loan or contact data on any server.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services. The only outside service is subscription checking (section 6).
  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not use your data for credit decisions, lending or marketing.

8. Keeping your data safe

Your records stay in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can turn on the app lock (Face ID, fingerprint or passcode). Backups are encrypted with your password before they leave the app. Because we have no copy of your data, keeping your phone and backup password safe is up to you.

9. Your control and deleting data

  • You can edit or delete any loan in the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.
  • We hold no loan or contact data about you. RevenueCat holds the anonymous purchase records described in section 6; to have them deleted, contact us (section 11).

10. Children

Lend Manager is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
